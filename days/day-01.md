# Day 1 — LLM Lifecycle and Project Goal

**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Goal

Understand the lifecycle of an LLM-backed system and define a measurable learning
project: build small components, test them, document limitations, and incrementally
connect them into a reliable AI application.

## Lifecycle notes

The five workbook stages are **pretraining**, **finetuning**, **RLHF**, **tool use**,
and **security**. In an engineering lifecycle, they sit inside problem definition,
data governance, evaluation, reproducible deployment, monitoring, and iteration.
